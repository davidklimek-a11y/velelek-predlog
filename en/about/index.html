About us

Velelek d.o.o. is a Belgrade company for manufacturing, trade and services. We started in 2001 as a wholesaler of veterinary medicines and went on to master the production of powdered medicines, vitamin tablets, liquid preparations and ointments.

People

We have 15 employees, six of them veterinarians and one a pharmacist. The company is led by Dejan Pejović, DVM, PhD, a specialist in industrial pharmacy. Our managers are veterinarians with field experience who know both production and distribution.

We take part in professional conferences on veterinary medicine and pharmacy in Serbia, and we fund our employees' postgraduate studies in industrial pharmacy at the Faculty of Pharmacy in Belgrade.

Wholesale and exports

Besides our own production, we work with many domestic manufacturers, agents and distributors of veterinary medicines and premixes. Our wholesale range has more than 1,000 articles, delivered to every part of Serbia.

Our distribution network supplies veterinary stations, clinics, pharmacies and institutions, pet shops and on-farm veterinary services. We export to North Macedonia, Bosnia and Herzegovina, Montenegro and Croatia.
