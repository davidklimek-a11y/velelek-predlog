Production

Our preparations are made in a 1,000 m² plant in Gornji Milanovac, Serbia, which we built and equipped in 18 months, from 2014 to 2015.

Video

The plant

Construction started in 2014 and the investment was completed 18 months later: a facility for non-sterile solid and liquid forms and powdered medicines, with a warehouse, built and equipped. We obtained all the licences the law requires to manufacture and trade medicines.

Our aim in production is preparations that are high in quality, safe, effective and affordable. We keep investing in equipment and in our people's knowledge and follow developments in the field, because protecting animal health also protects human health.
