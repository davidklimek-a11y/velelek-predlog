Description

The preparation is a dietary supplement intended for animals with problematic skin and hair cover. It is used for: improving the quality of hair and skin, in cases of dry and damaged hair, without shine, which is brittle and fragile, in disorders of pigmentation, irritation and inflammation of the skin. Positive effects can be noticed in the second week of application. Omega-3 fatty acids have anti-inflammatory and anti-edematous function. They reduce the need to use glucocorticosteroids used in the treatment of pruritus. B vitamins help the action of omega 3 fatty acids, and biotin reduces the increased secretion of sebaceous glands, which is important in seborrhea.

Method of administration

1 tablet daily per 10 kg of body weight
