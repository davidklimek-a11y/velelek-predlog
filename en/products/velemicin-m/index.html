Composition

1 gram of powder contains: Maduramicin 2 mg; retinol palmitate (vitamin A) 3000 IU; cholecalciferol (vitamin D3) 300 IU; Tocopherol acetate (vitamin E), 1 mg; menadione (vit. K3) 1 mg; Riboflavin (vit. B2) 3 mg; Nicotinamide (vit. B3) 13 mg; pyridoxine (vit. B6) 1 mg; vit. B12 2 mg; vit. C 2 mg;soluble carrier to 1g.

Indications

The combination of fat-soluble (A, D3, E) and a water-soluble vitamin C affects the maintenance of normal structure and function of the epithelium, maintenance and improvement of function germline, participates in the metabolism of carbohydrates, fats, minerals (calcium and phosphorus), prevents oxidation and prolongs biological half-life half-saturated fatty acids, ensures stability of cell membranes, stimulate growth, reproduction and embryonic development. The product is recommended during stressful situations in poultry, vaccination, transportation, diseases. Recommended preventive for poultry (chickens, turkeys), and for adult animals. Maduramicin is a product that is excellent in combating coccidiosis in poultry caused by E. mívat, E. Burnett, E. tenella, E. acervulina, E. maxima, E. necatrix and other. It works with diarrhea and other pathological conditions.

Contraindications

None known.

Side effects

not observed.

Dosage and administration

1 teaspoon per 3 liters of water. Oral use.

Instructions for proper use

It is used orally and 1 teaspoon per 3 liters of water for 5 consecutive days, which is enough for 25 individuals average weight of 1 kg. The product is administered in the food and drinking water for 5 consecutive days, and longer if necessary.

Withdrawal period

The product should not be used seven days before slaughter, eggs do not use 3 days after using the product.

Special warnings

This product is not a medicine. Store in a dry place at 25 ° C. Exp.date:3 years.
