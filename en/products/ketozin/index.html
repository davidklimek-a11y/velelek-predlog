Label and directions

The text of the product label. Read the pack leaflet before use.

Composition

1 ml of solution contains: 830 mg of propylene glycol; DL-methionine 18 mg; cobalt (II) sulfate heptahydrate, 0.1 mg; manganese (II) sulphate monohydrate, 0.2 mg; copper (II) sulphate pentahydrate 0.15 mg. Excipients phosphoric acid, purified water.

Indications

The preparation is applied in the state of ketosis in cows.

Contraindications

None known.

Side effects

not observed.

Dosage and administration

Dose is about 300-500 ml per animal. Oral application.

Instructions for proper use

The preparation is used by adding to drinking water or food. In order to achieve a better effect with the product is recommended giving glucose. If in the preparation appears white precipitate due to the holding in a cold place, it needs to be heated at a temperature of about 50 ° C and well protests until lost precipitate.

Withdrawal period

0 days.

Special warnings

This product is not a medicine. Store in a dry place at a temperature of 25 ° C. Keep out of reach of children.

Shelf life

3 years.
