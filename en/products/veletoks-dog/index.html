Label and directions

The text of the product label. Read the pack leaflet before use.

Composition

Permethrin cis: trans 25:75 1%.

Indications

Powder intended for the control of ticks, fleas, lice and other insects that parasitize dogs and their surroundings.

Contraindications

None known.

Side effects

Not observed.

Dosage and administration

5-7 g per square meter of the area of the room where the dogs stay, as well as at the root of the hair of the groin and armpits, neck, ears and roots of the tail.

Instructions for proper use

Sprinkle the powder evenly on the places where the insects appear. Wait 48-72 h for complete insect control. After that, clean the space that can be accessible to children with a vacuum cleaner. Protective measures are not required for single use, wear protective equipment for frequent use.

Withdrawal period

Not applicable.

Special warnings

This product is not a medicine. Store in a dry place at a temperature of 25 ° C. Exp. Date: 3 years. The preparation is toxic to aquatic organisms. May cause allergic skin reactions.

First aid

in case of inhalation, get out into the fresh air, rinse contact with eyes carefully with water for a few minutes, in case of ingestion, rinse mouth with water and in case of skin irritation seek medical advice.

Dispensing

The product is a non-prescription.
