Label and directions

The text of the product label. Read the pack leaflet before use.

Composition

Benzyl benzoate, thyme tincture.

Indications

It is used to treat skin diseases in large and small animals, most often as a result of ectoparasite infestations (fleas, scabies, demodex), but due to its mild antiseptic effect, it also protects the skin in other infections.

Contraindications

Do not use in cats and do not treat large areas of skin.

Side effects

Not observed with proper use.

Dosage and administration

The preparation is intended for external use. Apply the preparation to the affected area twice a day on the first day, and then carry out the treatment once a day until it recovers.

Instructions for proper use

The part of the skin to be treated should be washed with soap and warm water and dried. After that, carefully apply the preparation and rub it on the affected area and the skin around it. Make sure that the emulsion does not get to the eyes of animals.

Withdrawal period

Not applicable.

Special warnings

This product is not a medicine. Store in a dry place at a temperature up to 25 ° C in the original packaging. Keep out of reach of children.

Dispensing

The preparation is dispensed without a prescription from a veterinarian.
