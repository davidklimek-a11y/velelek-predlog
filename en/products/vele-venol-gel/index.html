Label and directions

The text of the product label. Read the pack leaflet before use.

Composition

Water, Carbomer, Isopropyl alcohol, Propylene glycol, Chestnut extract, Yarrow (Hajduk herb) extract, Menthol, TEA, Methylisothiazolinone, Methylchloroisothiazolinone

Indications

Refreshing vein gel based on chestnut and yarrow. Active components from chestnut and yarrow reduce capillary damage, improve venous tone, improve circulation, increase connective tissue elasticity, nourish the skin and reduce leg pain. The gel relieves problems with varicose and inflamed veins, relieves capillary rupture, reduces swelling and the feeling of tired and heavy legs.

Contraindications

It must not be used on damaged skin, it must not come into contact with eyes, mucous membranes and open wounds.

Side effects

Not noticed.

Dosage and administration

Externally, on the skin.

Instructions for proper use

rub 2-3 times a day with a gentle massage on the desired area in a thin layer.

Special warnings

This product is not a medicine. Store in a dry place at temperatures up to 25 ° C

The preparation is dispensed without a prescription.
