Composition

1g of powder contains: 400 mg of fodder yeast, mineral and vegetable carriers up to 1000 mg. Velefeks contains a minimum of 20% protein, Vit. B1 6 mg / kg, Vit. B2 28 mg / kg, Vit. B6 8 mg / kg.

Indications

Velefeks is intended for young animals, animals with high productivity, and breeding animals. Increases the general resistance of the body in stressful situations, enhances appetite, improves their growth and productivity. Yeast contains easily digestible proteins of favorable amino acid composition, growth factors, and the vitamin B complex.

Contraindications

None known.

Side effects

not observed.

Dosage and administration

Into complete mixture added 1-2% velefeks, for the all animals. Oral application.

Instructions for proper use

The preparation should be applied so that the contents of the bag mixed with 50 kg of food.
