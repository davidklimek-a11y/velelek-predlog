Our own plant in Gornji Milanovac

We started building the 1,000 m² plant in 2014 and had it finished and equipped within 18 months. It produces non-sterile solid, liquid and powder preparations and houses our warehouse. We hold all the licences the law requires to manufacture and trade medicines.
